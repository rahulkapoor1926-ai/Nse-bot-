## What was verified, and what was not

Verified from official sources (links in `docs/nse/STAGE_A.md`): SAHI hosts,
token flow, scopes, market-data paths and fields, rate limits, scrip-master
format (official docs and the official `sahiapi` 0.3.0 SDK source); NSE 2026
F&O holidays (circular 212/2025); Tuesday expiries; SAHI brokerage and
statutory rates after 1 April 2026.

**Not verified:**
1. **No live SAHI connection was made.** The build container's network policy
   blocks every `*.sahi.com` host. The client is tested only against recorded
   response shapes. Run `sahi-check` on your machine to verify.
2. **Index names in the scrip master.** "Nifty 50" is from SAHI's sample; "Nifty
   Bank" is assumed. `sahi-check` fails clearly if it is wrong; fix `index_names`.
3. **Pre-April-2026 charges** (exchange 0.03503%, brokerage Rs 10) are marked
   UNVERIFIED in `costs_india.json`.
4. **trader-alerts-nse** was not available, so nothing from it was reused.

## The data blocker, plainly

No genuine historical NIFTY/BANKNIFTY option data with bid/ask is in this
project, so every strategy gets **INSUFFICIENT DATA** and the paper loop will
alert candidates but never enter. That is the rule working.

Reaching 10,000 independent examples is hard by construction. With one
opening-range trade per index per day, two indices give about 490 examples a
year. 10,000 needs roughly 20 years of intraday option quotes, which do not
exist in a weekly-expiry form, or a strategy that fires many non-overlapping
times a day (whose examples are then correlated within a day). Options:

- Record forward with `record` (about 75 bars x 22 contracts x 2 indices per
  session). It builds genuine bid/ask history but adds only ~490 ORB examples a year.
- Buy intraday option history with bid/ask from a data vendor and convert it to
  the dataset format in `data/store.py`.
- Decide whether the 10,000 rule should count occurrences per strike or per
  bar. The current code does not, because those overlap and would inflate the count.

## Other limitations

- Polling REST every 15 seconds (quotes are limited to 1 request per second); no WebSocket feed yet.
- Muhurat and other special sessions are not traded.
- 2027 holidays must be added when NSE publishes them; until then the bot refuses to run in 2027.
- Backtests size one lot per trade; live sizing uses `budget.py`.
- Capital vs. limits: one NIFTY lot (65) with Rs 500 risk allows about 7 premium points of stop, and a round trip costs about Rs 45–50 before slippage. Slippage and gaps can make a loss larger than Rs 500. The Rs 2,000 daily figure is only a stop-for-the-day level.


## Stage G: stocks and ETFs (added 2026-10-30)

Scope widened to NSE stocks and ETFs, with options only when suitable.

| piece | module | notes |
|---|---|---|
| universe | `config/universe.example.json`, `instruments.load_universe`, `Master.bind_universe` | NSE EQ-series cash rows from the scrip master; unknown symbols refuse to start; ETF vs stock is declared in the universe file |
| charges | `costs.py`, `costs_india.json` | equity intraday and delivery from sahi.com/pricing: Rs 10 or 0.05% per order (lower), STT 0.025% sell intraday / 0.1% both delivery, DP Rs 13.5 per delivery sell, GST 18%. Rates before 2026-04-01 are ASSUMED equal to today's |
| liquidity | `quotes.check_for` | stocks: minimum price, top-of-book value, volume; no open-interest rule |
| sizing | `budget.size_shares` | whole shares, cash only, loss at stop + charges within the risk budget |
| scanning | `scanner.scan_equities` | pattern on each symbol's own bars; downside signals are skipped (no short selling) |
| backtest | `backtest/equity_engine.py` | fills at recorded bid/ask, or close +/- half an assumed spread; baseline = random entry time |
| history | `data/history.py` (`fetch-history`) | SAHI candles -> dataset marked `estimated_spread`; how far back SAHI goes is not documented |
| recording | `data/recorder.py` | records stock/ETF bars with real bid/ask going forward (`build-dataset --kind equity`) |
| paper loop | `bot.py` | several strategies in one process; tried in order; first passing entry wins |

With 12 symbols and one long signal per symbol per day at most, a year of
history gives at most about 2,900 examples. In the synthetic tests, about half of
the signals were downside signals and were skipped. Reaching 10,000 needs a
larger universe or several years of candles, if SAHI serves that much. Signals
on the same day across stocks are correlated with the market, so 10,000 of
them are worth less than 10,000 unrelated trials.
